Unzip this folder, then in it open a Command Prompt and run:
  bomberman.exe host              (you host; allow it through the firewall)
  bomberman.exe join 192.168.x.x  (join the host's IP)
  bomberman.exe join 192.168.x.x udp  (same, over UDP; allow UDP 8080 on the host)
  bomberman.exe                   (solo)
Arrows/WASD move, Space bombs, hold Shift to freeze in place and block others, M mutes music, N switches track, Escape quits.
